Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 • Data Controller: Socialeo Digital Studio • Lead Officer: Manpreeth N (Founder & CEO)
Summary: Socialeo operates with a strict privacy-first principle. We do not sell your personal data. We collect only what is strictly necessary to deliver high-performance web engineering, design systems, and communications.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Socialeo Digital Studio ("Socialeo", "we", "us", or "our"), led by Founder & CEO Manpreeth N (Manu), collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("User", "you") of the website https://socialeo.vercel.app/ and all associated client portals, digital assets, and development services.

We comply with international and regional privacy standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023 of India.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: directly provided by you, and automatically through interaction with our platform:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Inquiries: Full Name, Email Address, WhatsApp / Phone Number, and Project Scope transmitted via our Contact and Inquiry forms.
  • Client Portal & Billing Data: Entity Name, GSTIN / Tax ID, Billing Address, and designated technical points of contact for invoice generation.
  • Direct Communications: Any messages, feedback, or files shared via WhatsApp, Email, or direct consultation.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device screen resolution, and preferred theme (light/dark mode).
  • Usage Metrics: Page visit timestamps, referral URLs, scroll depth, and interaction with interactive modules (e.g. A4 capabilities brochure, project modals).

3. Legal Basis for Data Processing (GDPR & DPDP)

Processing Purpose Data Categories Legal Basis
Fulfilling project contracts & client billing Name, Company, Email, Phone, Address Performance of a Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b))
Responding to WhatsApp & website inquiries Name, Phone number, Message content Explicit Consent & Legitimate Interest
Security, spam prevention & CDN performance IP address, Browser telemetry, Session logs Legitimate Interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))
Tax compliance, invoicing & accounting audits Transaction records, Invoices, Proof of payment Legal Obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c))

4. Data Storage, Retention & Security

Socialeo implements enterprise-grade technical and organizational security controls:

  • Padlock HTTPS Encryption: End-to-end 256-bit TLS encryption across all edge endpoints.
  • Zero Data Brokerage: We never sell, rent, monetize, or barter your personal information to third parties or ad networks.
  • Retention Limits: General contact inquiries are retained for up to 24 months. Active client billing and contractual archives are retained in accordance with statutory accounting retention laws (7 years).

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you hold statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a full copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your data where legally permissible.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Opt out of communications or revoke previously granted consent at any time.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable JSON format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer directly at socialeopvtltd@gmail.com. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

To deliver sub-second performance, we partner with industry-leading cloud infrastructure providers who adhere to strict data processing standards:

  • Vercel / Cloudflare: Edge hosting, CDN caching, and DDoS mitigation.
  • Google Fonts: Typography rendering.
  • WhatsApp Business API / Meta: Direct messaging routing when initiated by you.
