Transparency & Privacy

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 • Operator: Socialeo Digital Studio • Compliance: GDPR • ePrivacy • DPDP 2023
Summary: Socialeo uses minimal, privacy-conscious local storage and essential cookies strictly to remember your preferred visual theme (light/dark mode) and secure admin login sessions. We do not use third-party cross-site advertising cookies.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are small text files or key-value data stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. They help web applications remember your preferences and deliver seamless navigation.

2. Cookies & Local Storage Keys We Use

Storage Key / Cookie Type & Purpose Duration
socialeo-theme Preference: Remembers whether you prefer Dark Mode or Light Mode Persistent (Local Storage)
socialeo_sidebar_collapsed UI Functionality: Remembers your sidebar expand/collapse preference Persistent (Local Storage)
socialeo_admin_pane_collapsed UI Functionality: Remembers Bill Management column state Persistent (Local Storage)
socialeo_admin_session Strictly Necessary: Authenticated studio session token for verified administrators Session / 30 Days

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Unlike traditional ad-supported agencies, Socialeo does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling scripts. We do not monetize your browsing history.

4. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View which cookies and local storage items are stored on your device.
  • Block cookies from specific sites or block all third-party cookies.
  • Clear all local cache data upon closing your browser.

Please note that disabling local storage may cause the website to revert to default Dark Mode upon page refresh.
